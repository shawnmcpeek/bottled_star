## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Inject nuclei into a sealed chamber. Fuse them up the stellar ladder. Keep the core alive without cracking the rim.

AIM · CHARGE · INJECT
Drag to aim, hold to charge, release to fire. Light elements fall hard; heavy ones crowd the center. Same-tier pairs and helium captures climb from hydrogen toward iron—the end of the line, where fusion stops paying.

CLASSIC
Build toward iron. Manage pressure as the chamber fills. End quietly as a white dwarf, or go loud when iron meets iron and the core collapses.

COLLAPSE
Every supernova leaves a neutron-star remnant behind. Remnants sink, pile up, and refuse to fuse. Drive two into each other hard enough—usually with another blast—and you get a kilonova: gold, platinum, and a little room to breathe. Score by kilonovas first, efficiency second. When you’ve banked enough, let it go quiet.

WHAT YOU’LL FEEL
• Dense, tactile physics in a sealed chamber
• A real fusion ladder (H → He → C → … → Fe), not random merges
• Endings that remember how your star died
• Separate boards for Classic and Collapse

Physicists call a fusion reactor “a star in a bottle.” Here, you hold it.
```
